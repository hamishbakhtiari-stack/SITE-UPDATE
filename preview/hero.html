Palette and contrast

The brand palette is fixed, so this is a verification pass rather than a colour proposal. Every pairing used in the hero was measured against WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • Teal dark#14615AButton hover and active
  • Gold#C9A84CStar glyphs, Save chip fill
  • Cream#F9F6F0Hero ground
  • Ink#1C1C1CHeadline, price, labels

Headline variants to test

Same layout, one string swapped. Worth running once traffic is steady enough to read a result.

  • A · shipping

    Sit longer without pain — in 7 days.

    Outcome plus a timeframe. Keeps the 7-Day Reset as the differentiator, but see the claims note below.

  • B · pain-led

    Your chair isn't the problem. Your lower back is.

    Speaks to the buyer who already spent on a chair that didn't fix it. Carries no health-outcome claim.

  • C · objection-led

    You've tried the $1,000 chair. Try the $117 fix.

    Puts the price anchor in the headline itself. Strongest against the "another cushion" objection.

Before you publish

"In 7 days" is a health-outcome claim, and risk register item R2 is still open. With no named physiotherapist backing the program, that phrasing is exposed on two fronts: Meta's health-claims policy, and ACCC rules on misleading representations. The layout does not depend on the claim — swapping in variant B or C leaves the design untouched. This is a copy decision, not a design blocker.

The rating block ships switched off. It renders only when show_rating is enabled in the theme editor, so no star count is hardcoded. Turn it on once there is real review data behind it.
